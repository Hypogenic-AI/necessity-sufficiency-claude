# Downloaded Papers

## Core Papers on Necessity, Sufficiency, and Selectivity

1. **The Quest for the Right Mediator** (mueller2024_quest_right_mediator.pdf)
   - Authors: Aaron Mueller, Jannik Brinkmann, Millicent Li, et al.
   - Year: 2024
   - arXiv: 2408.01416
   - Why relevant: Central survey organizing MI through causal mediation analysis; explicitly identifies selectivity as undermeasured

2. **How to Use and Interpret Activation Patching** (heimersheim2024_activation_patching_interpret.pdf)
   - Authors: Stefan Heimersheim, Neel Nanda
   - Year: 2024
   - arXiv: 2404.15255
   - Why relevant: Explicit framework mapping denoising→sufficiency, noising→necessity; AND/OR gate analysis

3. **Towards Best Practices of Activation Patching** (zhang2023_activation_patching_best_practices.pdf)
   - Authors: Fred Zhang, Neel Nanda
   - Year: 2023
   - arXiv: 2309.16042
   - Why relevant: Empirical comparison of corruption methods and metrics; STR vs GN findings

## Benchmarks

4. **CausalGym** (arora2024_causalgym.pdf)
   - Authors: Aryaman Arora, Daniel Jurafsky, Christopher Potts
   - Year: 2024
   - arXiv: 2402.12560
   - Why relevant: Introduces selectivity metric via control tasks; benchmarks 7 causal interpretability methods

5. **MIB: A Mechanistic Interpretability Benchmark** (mueller2025_mib_benchmark.pdf)
   - Authors: Aaron Mueller, Atticus Geiger, Sarah Wiegreffe, et al.
   - Year: 2025
   - arXiv: 2504.13151
   - Why relevant: Two-track benchmark with CPR (sufficiency) and CMD (necessity) metrics; SAEs vs neurons finding

## Circuit Discovery and Features

6. **Sparse Feature Circuits** (marks2024_sparse_feature_circuits.pdf)
   - Authors: Samuel Marks, Can Rager, Eric Michaud, et al.
   - Year: 2024
   - arXiv: 2403.19647
   - Why relevant: SAE-based circuit discovery; faithfulness (sufficiency) and completeness (necessity) evaluation; SHIFT for selectivity

7. **Sparse Autoencoders Find Highly Interpretable Features** (cunningham2023_sae_interpretable_features.pdf)
   - Authors: Hoagy Cunningham et al.
   - Year: 2023
   - arXiv: 2309.08600
   - Why relevant: Foundational SAE work for feature-level interpretability

8. **AtP*: An Efficient Method for Localizing LLM Behaviour** (kramar2024_atp_star.pdf)
   - Authors: Janos Kramar et al.
   - Year: 2024
   - arXiv: 2403.00745
   - Why relevant: Efficient attribution patching with failure mode analysis

## Theoretical Foundations

9. **Causal Abstractions of Neural Networks** (geiger2021_causal_abstractions.pdf)
   - Authors: Atticus Geiger et al.
   - Year: 2021
   - arXiv: 2106.02997
   - Why relevant: Foundational causal abstraction framework for MI

10. **Causal Abstraction: A Theoretical Foundation** (geiger2023_causal_abstraction_foundation.pdf)
    - Authors: Atticus Geiger, Duligur Ibeling, Amir Zur, et al.
    - Year: 2023
    - arXiv: 2301.04709
    - Why relevant: Extended theoretical framework defining interchange intervention accuracy (IIA)

## Seminal Circuit Analysis Papers

11. **Locating and Editing Factual Associations in GPT** (meng2022_rome_locating_editing.pdf)
    - Authors: Kevin Meng et al.
    - Year: 2022
    - arXiv: 2202.05262
    - Why relevant: Introduced causal tracing; ROME editing method; pioneered activation patching methodology

12. **Interpretability in the Wild: IOI Circuit** (wang2022_ioi_interpretability_wild.pdf)
    - Authors: Kevin Wang et al.
    - Year: 2022
    - arXiv: 2211.00593
    - Why relevant: Largest end-to-end circuit reverse-engineering (26 heads, 7 classes); canonical necessity/sufficiency example

13. **Towards Automated Circuit Discovery** (conmy2023_automated_circuit_discovery.pdf)
    - Authors: Arthur Conmy et al.
    - Year: 2023
    - arXiv: 2304.14997
    - Why relevant: ACDC algorithm for automated circuit finding; scalability of circuit discovery

14. **Progress Measures for Grokking** (nanda2023_grokking_mechanistic.pdf)
    - Authors: Neel Nanda et al.
    - Year: 2023
    - arXiv: 2301.05217
    - Why relevant: Mechanistic understanding of grokking; circuit analysis methodology
